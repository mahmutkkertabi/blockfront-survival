BLOCKFRONT SURVIVAL

A blocky first-person survival game. Reach Day 5, collect 5 crystal ores, place 10 blocks, and defeat 8 night hostiles to win.

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · Space jump
  • Left click mine / use equipment · Right click place block
  • 1–6 or mouse wheel switch equipment · R reload · Esc pause
  • Watch out: hostile cubes appear at night.
