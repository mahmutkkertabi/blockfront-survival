BLOCKFRONT SURVIVAL

A blocky multiplayer survival world: build, mine, fight friends, and survive hostile mobs.

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · Space jump
  • Left click mine / attack · Right click place block
  • 1–6 or mouse wheel switch tools · R reload · Esc pause
  • Up to 5 players total: you and four friends.
  • Hostile mobs: zombie, skeleton, creeper, spider, and enderman.
